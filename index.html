Frequently Asked Questions

Is KidsDraw free to use without sign-up?

Yes, KidsDraw is 100% free with no registration, no sign-up, and zero ads interrupting your child's drawing flow.

Is it safe for toddlers and young children?

Absolutely. All drawing operations are processed 100% inside your local browser memory. No drawings, camera data, or personal information is ever uploaded to any server.

Can children use Apple Pencil or touch screens?

Yes! KidsDraw natively supports capacitive touch screens, Apple Pencil pressure sensitivity, tablets, smartphones, and regular desktop mice.

How do I save or print my child's artwork?

Simply click the 'Export' button in the toolbar. You can instantly download high-definition PNG images ready for color printing, framing, or sharing with family.

What special tools are included for kids?

KidsDraw includes thick wax crayons, smooth brush pens, cute animal & space stamps, an intuitive eraser, warm eye-protection ivory paper, and a one-click anti-accidental touch lock.
